# Project VSM-1

## Speculative Low-Pressure Evaporative Sheet Architecture for Exploration of High-Surface-Area Heat Rejection Regimes

---

## What Is This?

Project VSM-1 is an open speculative engineering research repository exploring whether extreme upstream water purification, falling-film hydrodynamics, and ultra-low aerodynamic resistance can enable new operating regimes for evaporative heat rejection.

This repository is not a product.

It is not a startup.

It is not a deployment-ready cooling system.

It is a directional research program designed to investigate whether sparse vertical sheet architectures can produce favorable tradeoffs relative to conventional packed cooling towers for high-density compute infrastructure.

Failure is considered a valid outcome.

The objective is to identify governing constraints, bottlenecks, and breakpoints rather than prove a predetermined conclusion.

---

## Core Thesis

Traditional evaporative cooling towers rely on dense fill media that maximize surface area at the expense of increasing aerodynamic resistance.

Project VSM-1 explores the opposite direction:

* Aggressive upstream water rectification
* Sparse, parallel falling-film sheet geometries
* Ultra-low pressure-drop airflow paths
* Large-diameter, low-speed air movement
* Open identification of physical failure modes

The central research question:

> Can extreme fluid purification unlock evaporative geometries that become impractical under conventional water chemistry constraints?

---

## What Problems Does This Explore?

The repository focuses primarily on five existential questions:

### 1. Falling-Film Stability

Can continuous sub-millimeter films remain stable across large vertical surfaces without collapsing into rivulets?

### 2. Biological Colonization

How rapidly do open atmospheric wet surfaces lose performance through biological growth?

### 3. Aerodynamic vs Purification Tradeoffs

At what point does purification complexity eliminate the gains from reduced aerodynamic resistance?

### 4. Drift and Entrainment Limits

Can sparse geometries retain water without requiring conventional heavy eliminators?

### 5. Economic Reality

Does system complexity ultimately outweigh thermodynamic improvements?

## Validation Philosophy

The architecture is treated as a falsifiable hypothesis.

Validation follows a staged progression:

### Stage 1

Single-sheet laboratory experiments.

### Stage 2

Multi-sheet cassette testing.

### Stage 3

Small-scale pilot systems.

### Stage 4

Macro-scale economic validation.

Each stage exists to eliminate assumptions.
